Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2025

Pathways is a career navigation platform built to help people find the right job and chart a meaningful career path. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info — your name and email address, provided when you sign in with Google OAuth.
  • Profile data — career history, skills, and preferences you choose to add to your profile.
  • Usage data — jobs you swipe on, career paths you view, and features you interact with.
  • Device info — browser type and general location, only if you choose to provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalise your job feed and career recommendations based on your profile and activity.
  • Connect you with relevant Guides and career paths that match your goals.
  • Improve the platform by understanding how features are used in aggregate.
  • Send product updates and announcements, only if you opt in.

3. What We Never Do

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We never share your data with employers without your explicit consent.
  • We never use your data for advertising or ad targeting.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure, hosted on industry-leading cloud providers with strict access controls.

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest to protect your information from unauthorised access.

5. Your Rights

  • Access — view your data at any time through your profile within the app.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at matt.monahan2@gmail.com.
  • Portability — request an export of your data at any time by contacting us.
